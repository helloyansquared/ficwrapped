How to get your AO3 Wrapped

  1. Add the bookmark

    Drag the purple ✦ AO3 Wrapped button above to your bookmarks bar. Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac) if you can't see the bar.

  2. Open AO3 and click it

    Go to archiveofourown.org, logged in as usual, and click the bookmark. A small FicWrapped box reads your History pages, about one a second.

  3. Open your Wrapped

    When it's done, click Open my Wrapped. Your cards open in a new tab, ready to save or share.

On an iPhone or iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code below.
  2. Bookmark this page: tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, and save it.
  3. Open your bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark, rename it AO3 Wrapped and replace the address with the code you copied.
  4. Open AO3, log in, open your bookmarks and tap AO3 Wrapped.
On Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code below.
  2. Tap ⋮ then ☆ to bookmark this page. Tap Edit, rename it AO3 Wrapped and paste the code into the URL field.
  3. Open AO3 and log in. Tap the address bar, type AO3 Wrapped and tap the bookmark in the suggestions (the one with the star).

The bookmark only loads a small script from this site. You can read it: c.js and core.js, or on GitHub.

No bookmarks? Paste your History instead

Open AO3 and go to your History (your name in the top menu, then History). Select the whole page, copy it, and paste it in the box. Do the same for page 2, 3 and so on, until you reach the start of the year.

You can also drop the data file the bookmark saves, or History pages you saved from your browser.

What your AO3 Wrapped counts

AO3 doesn't make a Wrapped of its own. FicWrapped builds one from your AO3 History, the list AO3 keeps of every work you open while logged in. These are the rules it follows, so you know what the numbers mean.

About FicWrapped

FicWrapped is a small fan-made tool by yesyanyan (YanSquared), who also makes FicToEpub, the free AO3 HTML to EPUB converter, and BotToBook, which turns Character.AI chats into PDF and EPUB books. It was first published on 25 November 2026. The code is open on GitHub.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Organization for Transformative Works or the Archive of Our Own. Please support AO3 directly if you can: donate to the OTW.

How do I get AO3 Wrapped on my phone?

You can make your AO3 Wrapped on a phone in two ways. Save FicWrapped's bookmark code as a bookmark in Safari or Chrome and tap it while you're on AO3, or copy your AO3 History pages and paste them into FicWrapped one at a time. Neither needs an app or your password.

Make my AO3 Wrapped

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Free, no password, nothing uploaded.

Jump to: iPhone or iPad · Android · No bookmarks

AO3 Wrapped on iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. On FicWrapped, tap Copy bookmark code.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, and save.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the bookmark you just made, rename it AO3 Wrapped, clear the address field and paste the code. Tap Done.
  4. Open archiveofourown.org, log in, open Bookmarks and tap AO3 Wrapped.
  5. When it's finished, tap Open my Wrapped, then Share this card to save cards to Photos or post them.

AO3 Wrapped on Android (Chrome)

  1. On FicWrapped, tap Copy bookmark code.
  2. Tap ⋮ then ☆ to bookmark the page, then tap Edit. Rename it AO3 Wrapped and paste the code into the URL field.
  3. Open AO3 and log in. Tap the address bar, type AO3 Wrapped, and tap the bookmark in the list (the one with the star). Chrome only runs bookmark code this way, not from the bookmarks screen.

No bookmarks: paste your History

  1. On AO3, open your History (your name in the menu, then History).
  2. Select all the text on the page and copy it.
  3. On FicWrapped, tap the paste box under “No bookmarks? Paste your History instead” and paste.
  4. Go to the next History page and repeat until you reach fics from before the year started, then tap Make my Wrapped.

Pasted text loses some detail: AO3's page doesn't say in plain text which tags are characters and which are additional tags, so the characters and tags cards are skipped. The bookmark gives the full set.

Questions

Can I make my AO3 Wrapped on my phone?

Yes. On iPhone Safari and Android Chrome you can save the bookmark code as a bookmark and tap it on AO3; the steps are on the home page. If that's fiddly, copy your History pages one at a time and paste them into FicWrapped instead.

Why does my Wrapped count fics I didn't finish?

AO3 History records every work you open, not every work you finish, and it shows each work's full word count. So the totals are fics and words you opened. Most AO3 Wrapped tools count the same way.

Is there an AO3 Wrapped that doesn't need my password?

Yes. Tools that run in your own logged-in browser, like bookmarklets and some extensions, or that read History pages you paste, never receive your AO3 password. FicWrapped works both ways. Websites and most Colab notebooks that log in for you do need it.

Can I export my AO3 History?

AO3 has no export button for your History or Marked for Later list. To keep a copy, save each History page from your browser, or use FicWrapped's free bookmark to read them all at once and save them as a CSV spreadsheet or a searchable offline page, with a link to each fic.

Make my AO3 Wrapped

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Free, no password, nothing uploaded.

Why keep a copy

  • AO3 only stores History while it's switched on, and Clear History can't be undone.
  • AO3 was offline for about two and a half weeks in late 2026; a copy on your own device doesn't depend on the site being up.
  • A spreadsheet lets you sort and filter your reading in ways AO3's pages don't.

Free: save the pages yourself

Open your History (see where to find it), then use your browser's File → Save Page As on each page, 25 works at a time. It needs no tools, but it's slow for a big History.

Free with FicWrapped

  1. Add the bookmark from the home page and open AO3 while logged in.
  2. Click the bookmark, choose All time as the year, and tick Also save my Marked for Later list.
  3. Click Read my History. Big histories take a few minutes; AO3 is read about one page a second.
  4. Open your Wrapped and click History CSV or Offline History page, under Keep a copy of your History.

What the export includes

For every work: last visited date, visit count, title, authors, fandoms, rating, categories, warnings, relationships, characters, additional tags, word count, chapters, complete or in progress, kudos, hits, language, whether it's marked for later or has an update, and the AO3 link. Deleted works are listed with their last-visited date only.

If you want the fics themselves, download them from AO3 and convert them with FicToEpub.

Questions

Why is my AO3 History empty?

AO3 only saves History while it's switched on. Check Preferences on AO3 and turn on History. If you cleared your History, those visits are gone and no tool can get them back.

Is FicWrapped free?

Yes, the whole Wrapped is free. An optional $3.99 Supporter Pack adds a printable poster and cards without the web address. Saving your History as a CSV or an offline page is free.

Can I also download the fics in my Wrapped?

FicWrapped links each fic in the History export to its AO3 page. To turn AO3's HTML downloads into an EPUB for Kindle, Kobo or Apple Books, use FicToEpub, a free converter by the same maker.

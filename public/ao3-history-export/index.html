Can I export my AO3 History?

AO3 has no export button for your History or Marked for Later list. You can see them at archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings while logged in. To keep a copy, FicWrapped's bookmark reads those pages in your browser and the Supporter Pack saves them as a CSV spreadsheet and a searchable offline page, with a link to each fic.

Where to see your AO3 History

Log in, click your name in the top menu, then History. The address is archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings. Marked for Later is a tab on the same page (?show=to-read). Each entry shows the work, the last time you opened it, whether there's an update, and how many times you've visited.

Why keep a copy

  • AO3 only stores History while it's switched on, and Clear History can't be undone.
  • AO3 has had outages and maintenance in 2026; a copy on your own device doesn't depend on the site being up.
  • A spreadsheet lets you sort and filter your reading in ways AO3's pages don't.

Export with FicWrapped

  1. Add the bookmark from the home page and open AO3 while logged in.
  2. Click the bookmark, choose All time as the year, and tick Also save my Marked for Later list.
  3. Click Read my History. Big histories take a few minutes; AO3 is read about one page a second.
  4. Open your Wrapped. With the Supporter Pack, click History CSV or Offline History page.

What the export includes

For every work: last visited date, visit count, title, authors, fandoms, rating, categories, warnings, relationships, characters, additional tags, word count, chapters, complete or in progress, kudos, hits, language, whether it's marked for later or has an update, and the AO3 link. Deleted works are listed with their last-visited date only.

Free alternative

You can also save each History page from your browser (File → Save Page As) or copy them into a document. It's slower, but it needs no tools at all. If you want the fics themselves, download them from AO3 and convert them with FicToEpub.

Questions

Why is my AO3 History empty?

AO3 only saves History while it's switched on. Check Preferences on AO3 and turn on History. If you cleared your History or turned it off earlier in the year, those visits are gone and no tool can get them back.

Is FicWrapped free?

Yes, the whole Wrapped is free. An optional $3.99 Supporter Pack adds a printable poster, cards without the web address, and a CSV and offline copy of your loaded History.

Can I also download the fics in my Wrapped?

FicWrapped links each fic in the History export to its AO3 page. To turn AO3's HTML downloads into an EPUB for Kindle, Kobo or Apple Books, use FicToEpub, a free converter by the same maker.

Does FicWrapped need my AO3 password?

No. You log in to AO3 yourself, on AO3, as usual. The bookmark runs in that AO3 tab, reads only your History pages and passes the list to the FicWrapped tab you open. It never sees your password or cookies, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

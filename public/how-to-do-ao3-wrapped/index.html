How do I do AO3 Wrapped?

AO3 doesn't make a Wrapped, so you build one from your AO3 History. With FicWrapped: drag the AO3 Wrapped bookmark to your bookmarks bar, open AO3 while logged in, click the bookmark, wait while it reads your History, then click Open my Wrapped. Your cards open in a new tab, ready to save or share.

Make my AO3 Wrapped

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Free, no password, nothing uploaded.

Before you start

  • Be logged in to AO3 in this browser.
  • Make sure History is switched on (AO3 Preferences → Turn on History). AO3 can't show visits it never saved.
  • Use a computer if you can. On a phone, see AO3 Wrapped on your phone.

Step by step

  1. Add the bookmark. On the FicWrapped home page, drag the purple ✦ AO3 Wrapped button to your bookmarks bar. If you can't see the bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
  2. Open AO3. Go to archiveofourown.org and log in as usual. Any AO3 page works.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small FicWrapped box appears in the corner of AO3. Pick the year (2026 is the default), and tick Also save my Marked for Later list if you want it in your export.
  4. Click Read my History. The box reads your History pages one at a time, about one a second, and stops when it reaches fics you last opened before the year started.
  5. Click Open my Wrapped. A new tab opens with your cards. Tap the right side of a card to go forward. Use Share this card on a phone, or Save image and Save all cards on a computer.

What's in your Wrapped

Up to twelve cards: how many fics and words you opened, your top fandoms, ships, characters and additional tags, how your ratings split, the fic you visited most, your longest fic and how many were works in progress, your reading by month, your reader type, and a summary card laid out like an AO3 work header.

If something goes wrong

  • “Log in to AO3 first”: you're logged out in this tab. Log in and click the bookmark again.
  • “AO3 asked for a short break”: AO3 is rate-limiting. Leave the tab open; FicWrapped waits and continues.
  • The new tab says the AO3 tab didn't answer: go back to AO3, click Save data file, and drop that file on the FicWrapped page.
  • Nothing happens when you click the bookmark: some browser extensions block bookmarklets. Try another browser, or paste your History instead.

How the numbers are counted

How FicWrapped counts your AO3 History (checked 25 November 2026)
Where the data comes fromYour AO3 History, the page at archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings. AO3 lists 20 works per page, most recently visited first, and only shows it to you while you're logged in.
Which fics count for 2026A fic counts for 2026 if you last opened it in 2026. AO3 keeps only the latest visit date, so a fic you read in March and reopened in October counts in October.
FicsEach work counts once, however many times you opened it. Works you opened but didn't finish still count: AO3 records visits, not finished reads.
WordsThe work's full word count as shown on AO3, not the chapters you actually read.
VisitsAO3's own "Visited N times" counter for each work, which is how the most-reread card is chosen.
Deleted worksCounted separately. AO3 keeps only the date for them, so they aren't in the fandom or tag totals.
Reading timeTotal words ÷ 250 words a minute.
SpeedThe bookmark reads about one History page a second. If AO3 asks it to slow down, it waits and carries on.
What you needAn AO3 account, logged in, with History switched on (AO3 Preferences → Turn on History).

Questions

Is there an official AO3 Wrapped?

No. AO3 doesn't make a personal year-in-review. Every AO3 Wrapped is fan-made from your AO3 History. FicWrapped is one of them: it reads your History in your own browser and turns it into shareable cards, without asking for your password.

How do I do AO3 Wrapped?

Drag the AO3 Wrapped bookmark from ficwrapped.pages.dev to your bookmarks bar, open AO3 while logged in and click it. It reads your History pages for the year, then you click Open my Wrapped to see and save your cards. It takes about a minute for most readers.

How long does it take?

About one second per History page of 20 fics, so 400 fics take around 20 seconds. If AO3 asks it to slow down, FicWrapped waits and continues on its own. You can also stop early and use what's loaded.

Why is my AO3 History empty?

AO3 only saves History while it's switched on. Check Preferences on AO3 and turn on History. If you cleared your History or turned it off earlier in the year, those visits are gone and no tool can get them back.

Why do some months look empty?

AO3 keeps only the last time you visited each work. If you read a fic in February and reopened it in November, it shows up in November. Fics you removed from your History, or read while logged out or with History off, aren't counted.

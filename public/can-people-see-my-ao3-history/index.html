Can other people see my AO3 History?

No. AO3 lets only the account owner open their History, and only while logged in, at archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings. It lists each work you opened, the last date you visited it and how many times. You can delete single entries, clear it, or switch it off in Preferences.

Where to find your AO3 History

Log in, click your name in the top menu and choose History, or go to archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings. AO3 shows 25 works per page, most recently visited first. Marked for Later is a tab on the same page.

What AO3 History records

  • Each work you opened while logged in, once.
  • The last date you visited it, and how many times you've visited.
  • Whether the work has been updated since you last read it.
  • Works that were later deleted, as a date only.

It doesn't record finished reads, and it doesn't record anything while you're logged out.

Delete, clear or switch off History

  • One entry: click Delete from History under the work.
  • Everything: click Clear History at the top of the page. AO3 warns this can't be undone.
  • Stop recording: Preferences → untick Turn on History. Works you visited while it was on are kept; switch it back on to see them.

Keep a copy

AO3 has no export button for History. To save one, see how to export your AO3 History. To see a year of it as cards, make your AO3 Wrapped.

Questions

Can other people see my AO3 History?

No. AO3 only lets the account owner open their History, and only while logged in. It lists each work you opened, the last date you visited it and how many times. You can delete single entries, clear it, or switch it off in Preferences.

Where is my AO3 History?

Log in, click your name in the top menu and choose History, or go to archiveofourown.org/users/your-name/readings. Marked for Later is a tab on the same page.

If I switch History off, is my old History deleted?

No. AO3 says works you visited while History was on are still saved after you switch it off; you just have to switch it back on to see them. To remove them, use Clear History, which can't be undone.

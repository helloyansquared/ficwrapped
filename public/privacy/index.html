Privacy & refunds

FicWrapped reads your AO3 History and makes your Wrapped inside your own browser. Your History is never uploaded to us, stored by us or seen by us. This page lists the few things that do leave your device.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Your AO3 History

The bookmark runs in your AO3 tab, fetches your own History pages from AO3, and passes the list straight to the FicWrapped tab with the browser's postMessage. Pasted or dropped pages are read by JavaScript on this page. Your latest Wrapped data is kept in your browser's local storage so it's still there after a reload; clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your AO3 login

FicWrapped never asks for your AO3 password and the bookmark doesn't read your cookies. You stay logged in to AO3 the normal way.

License keys

When you activate the Supporter Pack, your license key is sent to a small checking service (a Cloudflare Pages Function) that asks Dodo Payments whether the key is valid for FicWrapped. The key and an activation ID are saved in your browser's local storage. The checking service doesn't keep the key and never receives your History.

Payments

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, which handles your card details, sales tax and receipts under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. FicWrapped uses no cookies, analytics or ads.

Refunds

If the Supporter Pack doesn't work for you, email hello@yansquared.com within 14 days of buying and you'll get a refund.

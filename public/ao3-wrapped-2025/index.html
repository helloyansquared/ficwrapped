Can I still get my AO3 Wrapped for 2025?

Partly. AO3 keeps only the last date you visited each work, so a 2025 Wrapped made now includes only fics you last opened in 2025. Anything you reopened in 2026 has moved to 2026. In FicWrapped, choose 2025 in the Year menu, or All time to include everything in your History.

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Free, no password, nothing uploaded.

Why last year's Wrapped is incomplete

AO3 History has one line per work with the last time you visited it and a visit count. It doesn't keep a list of every visit. So:

  • You read a fic in June 2025 and never opened it again: it counts for 2025.
  • You read a fic in June 2025 and reopened it in March 2026: it now counts only for 2026.
  • You deleted it from your History, or cleared your History: it's gone from both years.

The more you reread, the more of your 2025 has moved into 2026.

How to make it

  1. Add the bookmark from the home page and open AO3 while logged in.
  2. Click the bookmark and choose 2025 in the Year menu, or All time for your whole History.
  3. Click Read my History. Earlier years take longer, because the bookmark reads through 2026 first.
  4. Click Open my Wrapped.

Make next year's complete

Save a copy of your History in late December, before you start rereading in January. The History export page shows how.

Questions

Can I make an AO3 Wrapped for last year?

Partly. Choose 2025 in the Year menu. Because AO3 keeps only the last date you visited each work, fics you reopened in 2026 have moved to 2026, so a 2025 Wrapped made now is incomplete.

Why do some months look empty?

AO3 keeps only the last time you visited each work. If you read a fic in February and reopened it in November, it shows up in November. Fics you removed from your History, or read while logged out or with History off, aren't counted.

Why is my AO3 History empty?

AO3 only saves History while it's switched on. Check Preferences on AO3 and turn on History. If you cleared your History, those visits are gone and no tool can get them back.

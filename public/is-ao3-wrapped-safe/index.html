Is AO3 Wrapped safe to use?

It depends on how the tool gets your History. Tools that ask for your AO3 password or session cookie can do anything your account can until you change your password. Tools that run in your own logged-in browser, or that you paste History into, never get your login. FicWrapped works the second way, and its code is public.

The three ways AO3 Wrapped tools work

AO3 Wrapped methods compared
You type your AO3 password into a website or notebookThe tool logs in as you. Whoever runs it could read or change anything on your account. If you've done this, change your AO3 password afterwards.
You copy your session cookie into a scriptSame access as your password until you log out of AO3 everywhere. Many Colab notebooks used this in past years; some stopped working when AO3's protection started blocking them.
A bookmark or extension runs in your AO3 tabThe code runs in your own browser with your existing login, and the tool's owner never receives your password or cookie. You're trusting the code itself, so use one you can read.
You paste or upload your History pagesThe tool only sees what you paste. Nothing runs on AO3 at all.

What FicWrapped's bookmark does

  • Loads two small files from ficwrapped.pages.dev: c.js (the box you see) and core.js (reads the pages).
  • Fetches only /users/your-name/readings pages, about one a second, plus Marked for Later if you tick it.
  • Passes the list to the FicWrapped tab you open with postMessage. No server receives it.
  • Doesn't read your password, cookies, messages, drafts or anything else on your account.

The source is on GitHub. If you'd rather not run any code on AO3, use paste mode.

General tips

  • Never give a third-party site your AO3 password.
  • If you already did, change your AO3 password; that also logs out other sessions.
  • Be wary of “AO3 Wrapped” links in comments or fics that ask you to log in on a page that isn't archiveofourown.org.

Questions

Does FicWrapped need my AO3 password?

No. You log in to AO3 yourself, on AO3, as usual. The bookmark runs in that AO3 tab, reads only your History pages and passes the list to the FicWrapped tab you open. It never sees your password or cookies, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Is it safe to run a bookmark on AO3?

A bookmark runs code in the AO3 tab, so only use ones you can read. FicWrapped's script is short, published at ficwrapped.pages.dev/c.js and on GitHub, and it only fetches your own History pages. If you'd rather not run any code on AO3, use the paste option.

Will AO3 block me for using it?

FicWrapped reads your History at about the pace of someone clicking Next, and it backs off when AO3 says to retry later. That's far gentler than downloading fics. If AO3 is down or under maintenance, wait and try again later.

Is there an official AO3 Wrapped?

No. AO3 doesn't make a personal year-in-review. Every AO3 Wrapped is fan-made from your AO3 History. FicWrapped is one of them: it reads your History in your own browser and turns it into shareable cards, without asking for your password.
